GentlyOS.io — Free Tier Acceptable Use Policies

Last updated 2026-05-28 · version v0 · crystal {policies_hash}

These policies sit on top of the Free Tier Terms. Reading and accepting both gives you a 30-day session token for the public read API.

What you may do

  • Call the public read API at the documented rate limit.
  • Embed the API output in your own apps, products, or research, with reasonable attribution where applicable.
  • Cache responses locally and re-use them; the crystal-anchored result cache on the substrate side serves the same purpose globally.

What you must not do

  • No weight extraction. Don't attempt to reconstruct, distill, or steal the underlying model weights through systematic probing.
  • No rate-limit evasion. Don't rotate IPs, rotate tokens, or otherwise circumvent the limits.
  • No competing-product training. Output from the Free Tier may not be used to train a model that competes with the operator's commercial products without a separate written license.
  • No safety-critical deployment. Don't deploy outputs into life-safety, medical, legal, or financial-advice contexts without independent professional review.
  • No illegal content generation (CSAM, targeted harassment, IP infringement, etc.).

Data we collect

  • Acceptance ledger: your handle or email (whichever you provide), IP prefix, and User-Agent prefix. Crystal-anchored for audit.
  • Inference records: every API call writes one record to the substrate's chain with tier='inference'. The prompt is hashed by default; opt-in to retain full prompts via a header flag (off by default).
  • Session token: HMAC-signed; held only by you after issuance.

Data we do not collect

  • No third-party advertising tracker on the developer pages.
  • No biometric data.
  • No personal data beyond what you submit at signup.

Reporting

Suspected misuse → send to the developer contact below. Responsible-disclosure process for vulnerabilities.
