These policies sit on top of the Terms of Service. Reading and accepting both gives you a session token good for 30 days on the trollz.fun surface.

What you may do

  • Browse the storefront, view minted NFTs, view prize pools.
  • Purchase TROLLN tokens through the disclosed payment rails (Stripe, Sui USDC).
  • Mine zNiXz / SYNTH by contributing compute, content curation, or signal validation through approved clients.
  • Mint your own NFTs and offer them for sale, paying the disclosed minting fees.
  • Use the public read API at the rate limit posted on the developer page.

What you must not do

  • No fraud or wash trading. Don't artificially inflate volume, manipulate prize-desk outcomes, or coordinate market actions to deceive other users.
  • No exploitation. If you discover a vulnerability, disclose it through the responsible-disclosure email below; do not exploit it.
  • No illegal content. No CSAM, no targeted harassment, no content that infringes intellectual property of others, no incitement to violence.
  • No automation of the public UI. Headless browsers, scraping bots, and rate-limit-evading clients are prohibited. Use the documented API instead.
  • No sanctions evasion. If you are located in or acting on behalf of a sanctioned jurisdiction or person, you may not use this service.

Data we collect

  • Acceptance ledger: your user_id (or wallet address), the IP prefix of the accepting request, and the User-Agent prefix. Crystal-anchored for audit.
  • Session token: HMAC-signed; held only by you after issuance.
  • Chain entries: when you mint, purchase, or transact, the on-chain receipt is public by chain design.
  • No third-party advertising trackers are loaded by the storefront itself.

Data we do not sell

We don't sell your personal data to third parties. Aggregate, anonymized statistics may be published as part of public economic reports about the ecosystem.

DMCA and takedowns

Send notices to the contact address. Counter-notices follow the standard 17 U.S.C. § 512 process.
